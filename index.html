02 / Selected hardware

Selected Hardware Design Work

Selected examples of recent embedded hardware, wireless connectivity and mixed-signal PCB design, supported by engineering documentation and project evidence.

CAD design & engineering evidence

EdgeLink S3 Cellular — ESP32-S3 Cellular IoT

A four-layer KiCad design integrating ESP32-S3 processing with SIM7080G LTE-M / NB-IoT connectivity and GNSS. USB-C, battery charging, power-path management and buck-boost regulation support the connected platform. Hierarchical schematics, PCB layout and engineering documentation bring together cellular interfaces, motion and environmental sensing, and manufacturing preparation.

ESP32-S3 · SIM7080G · LTE-M / NB-IoT · GNSS · KiCad

CAD design & engineering evidence

Nordic nRF52840 — BLE Sensor Hardware

A modular Nordic nRF52840 design integrating Bluetooth Low Energy, environmental and motion sensors, GNSS and battery power management. The XIAO-based architecture brings together sensor and expansion interfaces, KiCad schematic capture and PCB layout, component selection and manufacturing documentation. The portfolio also presents embedded firmware, custom BLE services and sensor-to-application demonstrations.

nRF52840 · BLE · XIAO · KiCad · Low-power design

STM32WL — LoRa & Mixed-Signal PCB Design

An STM32WLE5 mixed-signal design integrating analogue signal conditioning, ADC acquisition, sub-GHz LoRa RF and separate power domains on a four-layer PCB. Altium schematic capture and layout are supported by component selection, LTspice circuit simulation, Python signal analysis and engineering documentation covering system architecture, signal paths and PCB implementation.

03 / About

Engineering with the whole product in view.

I am an electronic systems and product-development engineer with over 25 years of experience spanning hardware architecture, analogue, digital and mixed-signal PCB design, embedded firmware, RF and cellular IoT.

My work combines hands-on development with end-to-end product ownership and engineering leadership: defining systems, verifying designs, preparing manufacturing documentation and supporting NPI and production.

Commercial experience includes FAAC RF hardware and volume manufacturing, Watt Footprint energy monitoring hardware and firmware with 25 pilot units, and AmbiSense low-power optimization and sensing.
